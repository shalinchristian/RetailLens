## ◉ THE IDEA

Retail businesses generate thousands of transactions, but raw transactions don't explain the business.

**RetailLens** takes that raw history and asks:

> **What is selling? Who is buying? Where is the revenue coming from? And how does it change over time?**

The answers come from cleaning, exploring, visualizing, and finally presenting the data through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## ◉ THE DATA

**Online Retail Dataset**
UCI Machine Learning Repository

Transaction-level data from a **UK-based online retailer**, containing invoice, product, quantity, price, date, customer, and country information.

**Source:** [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)

## ◉ WHAT RETAILLENS REVEALS

**SALES**
Revenue trends, orders, volume, and seasonal patterns.

**PRODUCTS**
Top-performing products by quantity and revenue.

**CUSTOMERS**
Purchasing behaviour and high-value customers.

**MARKETS**
Country-wise revenue and transaction distribution.

**DATA QUALITY**
Missing, duplicate, cancelled, and invalid records.
